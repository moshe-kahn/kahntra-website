Meal Match treats choosing what to eat as a shared decision shaped by real constraints.

Choosing what to eat is often a coordination and decision problem rather than a lack of options. Preferences, energy, dietary constraints, time, and the people involved all shape the choice.

Choosing what to eat is often a coordination and decision problem rather than a lack of options. A good choice has to work for preferences, dietary needs, time, energy, and the people involved.

Meal Match is an early concept for making food decisions around real constraints.

Meal Match is an early product concept for helping individuals or groups decide what to eat by narrowing options around preferences, context, and practical constraints.

Meal Match is an early product concept for helping individuals or groups decide what to eat. It would narrow a broad set of options through preferences, context, and practical constraints, helping one person or a group move from indecision toward a workable choice.
